WEBSITE INFORMATION

Website privacy

This page describes the public information website only. It is not the MyDugo mobile app’s privacy policy.

Information on this website

This website has no account sign-in, contact form, analytics code, or medical-information collection. Links to the app and to Philippine Red Cross pages take you to separate services with their own practices.

Hosting and external links

The website’s hosting provider may process standard connection information, such as IP address and request logs, to deliver and secure the site. We do not control the privacy practices of external sites linked here.

Mobile app privacy

The MyDugo mobile app handles account and health-related information in separate systems. An approved app privacy policy has not yet been published here. Please contact chapter staff for app privacy questions before sharing information.

Contact

For chapter contact details, see the contact section or the official Philippine Red Cross listing.
