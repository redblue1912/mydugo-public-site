01 / THE MISSION

Humanity in action,
across the Philippines.

The Philippine Red Cross works to protect life and dignity and build more resilient communities through humanitarian service.

Its work includes blood services, disaster management, safety, health, social services, and youth and volunteer services. It is guided by seven fundamental principles: humanity, impartiality, neutrality, independence, voluntary service, unity, and universality.

Its National Blood Services supports a safe blood supply and encourages voluntary blood donation. Explore the official site for current programs and guidance.

06 / GOOD TO KNOW

Common questions,
clear answers.

For personal medical questions, contact chapter staff or your healthcare provider.

Does the eligibility questionnaire approve me to donate?

No. It helps chapter staff review your information. Staff make the eligibility decision, and the donation center makes the final on-site assessment.

What happens if I need a blood test?

Follow the chapter’s instructions for a blood-test appointment or an accepted outside-lab report. Authorized staff review the result and record the Pass or Failed decision.

Will MyDugo send me notifications?

Updates appear in the in-app inbox. Device notifications depend on your device settings and app configuration. Do not rely on the app alone for urgent medical communication.

How do I install the Android app?

When an approved public release is available, use the verified download link on this page. Android may ask you to confirm installation from your browser. Download only from a link shared by the chapter.
